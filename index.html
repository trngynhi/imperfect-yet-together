About the Project

Jan Eliasson’s quote directly addresses inequality and emphasizes the importance of leaving no one behind, especially those facing the greatest disadvantages. Equality means having equal opportunities, being included in social and economic life, and having access to resources, which are still lacking in many communities. This is relevant to SDG 10, as it aims to support disadvantaged groups in society, bring people closer together, and reduce inequality. Inequality can exist anywhere and affect anyone, regardless of race, ethnicity, or background. In order to take action against this issue, people must first acknowledge and understand these situations, prioritize those who need support the most, and bring opportunities closer to them. Individuals need to start with themselves by spreading awareness, offering a helping hand, and actively seeking information about social issues. Treat others with dignity and respect, and be willing to change your mindset. Only then can we, as a community, work together to support others and take bigger actions, such as organizing events, providing community facilities, and more. Inequality can start with the smallest and can be changed with the smallest action, be the change today.
